Candidates (up to 12)

Save

Save the file as config/fields/candidates.geojson. Click to add a vertex. Click the first vertex, press Enter or Close polygon to close. Backspace undoes a vertex, Esc stops drawing. Drag to move the view, + - or pinch to zoom. Draw inside the field edges: a 30 m strip along the border is dropped and the field needs at least 20 pixels left. No owner, no CAR, no names: only the id is saved. The drawing is kept in this browser until you clear it.
